I read this book in January of 2023. It was incredible to see authoritarianism under a microscope, how characters were forced to deal with it, and how a state like that operated. Events these days and a lot of the things this administration does remind you of this book and have only made its message stronger and clearer over time.
As a child, I loved to watch a TLC show called Extreme Couponers. It seems fascinating to me that people built up their own stockpiles at home of canned foods, water, etc. This book had not only that survivalist aspect but also explored how religious fanaticism, isolation, and a lack of education can warp peoples' minds--and the struggle to remove yourself from an environment like that and make a better life for yourself.
Blown to Bits is possibly the greatest book ever written, and the greatest book I have ever read. As part of the summer assignment for AP Computer Science Principles, I read this book with an open mind and open heart. I was delighted to learn so many things about computing: binary, computer logic, and even cybersecurity. Writing summaries for each chapter, I gained a true appreciation for how the Internet works. I rave about this book to everyone I know, and I eagerly anticipate the next chance I'll have to recommend it to others.
